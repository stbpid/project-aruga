# Project Aruga Profiling Tool: Pretest Guide for Testers

Thank you for helping test the new version of the Profiling Tool.

## What is different about the pretest app

- It is a **separate app** on your laptop called **Project Aruga Pretest**.
- Every screen has an orange **PRETEST VERSION** bar at the top.
- What you enter is saved in **separate test records**. These are **not** official
  Project Aruga records and do not appear in the Management Dashboard.
- The ID shown at the end (for example `PRETEST-2026-R4A-0001`) is a **test ID**.
  Do **not** give it to the family, and it cannot be used for claiming benefits.
- Keep using the regular Profiling Tool (projectaruga.com) for real, official profiling.

## Installing

1. Download the installer your coordinator shares (`Project Aruga Pretest_..._setup.exe`).
2. Double-click it. If Windows says **"Windows protected your PC"**, click
   **More info → Run anyway**. This appears because this is a test app.
3. Open **Project Aruga Pretest** from the Start menu or the desktop.

## Using it

1. **Connect to the internet.** This version needs internet. Offline use comes later.
2. Enter your usual **8-character Interviewer Code**, choose the language, tick the
   Data Privacy box, and click **Start Profiling**.
   - If your code is not accepted, you may not be enrolled in the pretest yet.
     Contact your coordinator.
3. Fill in the form the same way as the regular tool.
4. Click **Submit** on the Review step.
5. After saving, click **Start a new profile** for the next family, or **End session**
   when you are done.

## Reporting a problem

While filling in the form, click the orange **Report a problem** button (bottom left).

- Write what was confusing, missing or wrong on that section.
  Example: *"Tenure Status list is missing 'Rent-free with consent of owner'."*
- **Do not type the family's personal details** (names, addresses, phone numbers) in feedback.
- You can report several problems. They are sent together with the profile when you submit.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Unable to reach the server" / "No internet connection" | Check your connection and click Submit again. Your answers stay on the screen. |
| "Session expired" | End the session, log in again, and re-enter the profile. |
| "Some required answers are missing" | Go back through the steps and complete the highlighted fields. |
| You are unsure whether it saved | Click Submit again. The app never saves the same profile twice. |
| Anything else | Take a screenshot and send it to your coordinator. |

## Privacy reminders

- You are handling personal information about children with disabilities (Data Privacy Act of 2012).
- Lock your laptop when you step away, and click **End session** when you finish.
- Do not share screenshots that show a family's details.
