DSWD Social Technology Bureau
User Guide

A simple, step-by-step guide to help DSWD social workers and interviewers use the Project Aruga profiling system with ease and confidence.

format_list_bulleted What's in this guide
login1. Getting Started key2. How to Log In assignment3. Filling Out the Form check_circle4. Reviewing & Submitting qr_code5. Aruga ID & QR Code lightbulb6. Tips & Common Questions
login

1. Getting Started

Before you begin conducting home visits and profiling beneficiaries, make sure you have the following ready:

check_circle

Your Interviewer Code

This is the 8-character code assigned to you by your supervisor. You will need this every time you log in. It looks like this: AB12CD34

check_circle

A Device with Internet Connection

The system works on smartphones, tablets, and computers. Use a modern browser like Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge for the best experience.

check_circle

The Beneficiary's Information

Have the household's 4Ps ID ready (if applicable), along with the child's personal details, health records, and family information.

info

Good to know: The system is designed for one beneficiary per session. After submitting, you can start a new session for the next beneficiary.

key

2. How to Log In

Follow these simple steps to access the profiling tool:

1

Open the Project Aruga website

Visit the system on your browser. You will see the login page with a Data Privacy Agreement on the right side.

2

Read and accept the Data Privacy Agreement

Scroll through the agreement text, then check the box. This is required before you can proceed.

3

Enter your Interviewer Code

Type your 8-character code in the field labeled Interviewer Code. The code will appear in uppercase automatically.

4

Click "Submit and Proceed"

The system will verify your code. If correct, you will be taken directly to the profiling tool.

warning

Important: Do not share your Interviewer Code with anyone. Each code is linked to your account and all submitted records will be tagged under your name.

assignment

3. Filling Out the Profiling Form

The profiling form is divided into 10 steps. You must complete each step before moving to the next.

1

Pre-Qualification

Asks if the beneficiary is a 4Ps member. If yes, enter their 18-digit Household ID.

2

Respondent Profile

Enter the details of the person you are interviewing — usually a parent or guardian.

3

Child Profile

Enter the child's complete name, address, date of birth, religion, IP group, educational level, disabilities, and critical illnesses.

4

Family Profile

List all household members — name, age, sex, civil status, occupation, and any disabilities.

5

Socio Economic

Describe the family's living situation — house materials, ownership, electricity, water, toilet, and garbage disposal.

6

Health

Vaccinations, ongoing conditions, monthly health expenses, and barriers to accessing care.

7

Education

Whether the child is enrolled in school, grade level, accessibility features, and learning support received.

8

Economic Capacity

Family's monthly income and its source. The system will classify the income level automatically.

9

Service Availment

Financial assistance received, awareness of social services, and challenges in accessing them.

10

Assessment Notes

Write your professional observations — Strengths, Assessment, and Recommended Actions. Select a Readiness Score to indicate urgency of intervention.

info

Validation: If you miss a required field, the system will highlight it in red. Fix all errors before moving to the next step.

check_circle

4. Reviewing & Submitting the Assessment

After completing all 10 steps, you will be taken to the Review Page — a full summary of everything you entered.

1

Check the Summary

Scroll through the review page and make sure all the information is correct.

2

Go Back if Needed

If you notice a mistake, click "Edit Forms" to go back and make corrections.

3

Click "Submit Assessment"

Once satisfied, click the blue "Submit Assessment" button. The system will save the record and take you to the Success page.

warning

Once submitted, you cannot edit the record. Please double-check all information before clicking Submit.

qr_code

5. The Aruga ID & QR Code

After a successful submission, the system generates a unique Aruga ID and a QR Code for the beneficiary.

print

Print the QR Code

Click "Print QR Code" to print it. Give the printout to the beneficiary's family for safekeeping.

download

Save a Digital Copy

Click "Save Digital Copy" to download the QR code as an image file.

qr_code_scanner

How the QR Code is Used

When scanned, it opens the beneficiary's verified profile page showing their Aruga ID and registration date.

info

New Beneficiary: Once done, click "New Beneficiary" on the success page to start a fresh session for the next family.

lightbulb

6. Tips & Common Questions

helpWhat is the session timer for?

The timer shows how long you have been in the current session. It does not log you out automatically.

helpWhat if I press the back button on my phone?

The system will show an "End Session?" warning to prevent you from accidentally losing your work.

helpA required field is highlighted red. What do I do?

Read the error message below the field, fix the input, and click Next again. All red fields must be resolved before you can proceed.

helpThe dropdown is empty / not loading?

Wait a few seconds and try again. Make sure you have a stable internet connection. If it continues, try refreshing the page.

helpCan I profile the same beneficiary twice?

The system will create a new record each time. Please coordinate with your supervisor to avoid duplicate submissions.

helpI need help. Who do I contact?

Reach out to the DSWD Social Technology Bureau at stb_projectaruga@dswd.gov.ph or call (02) 8931-8108 local 1234.
